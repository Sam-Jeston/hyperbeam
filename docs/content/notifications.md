title: Notifications
description: Hear from your agents when they need you, in Hyperbeam and by direct message in Slack or Discord.

Agents work without you watching. Hyperbeam tells you when one of yours needs you, so you do not have to keep its page open.

## What you are told

You get a notification when an agent **you started**:

- **asks you a question**,
- **finishes a turn** and is waiting to hear what is next, or
- **fails**.

Some things are left out on purpose:

- **Workers under a conductor** report to the [conductor](/conductor), not to you. You hear from the conductor.
- **A turn you interrupted** is not news to you.
- **A turn that ends with a question** is one notification, the question.
- **A turn that ends with your next message already queued** is not announced: the agent is not waiting.

Other people’s agents never notify you. The [board](/board) and **Activity** show what the whole team is doing.

## In Hyperbeam

**Notifications** in the sidebar lists them, newest first, with a count of the ones you have not read. Select one to open the agent’s page.

A notification is marked as read when you open the agent’s page or reply to the agent, from anywhere, including a chat thread. If you are already on the agent’s page when it happens, it arrives read. **Mark all read** clears the rest.

Notifications are kept for 30 days.

## In Slack or Discord

The [chat app](/chat) can also send you each notification as a direct message, with a button that opens the agent’s page. Turn this on for yourself under **Settings → My account → Notifications**. It needs two things first:

1. An admin has [connected](/chat#set-up-slack) the Slack or Discord app.
2. Your chat account is [linked](/chat#who-you-are) to you. Mention the bot and it will link you, or send you a link.

The message goes to every account you have linked on that platform. Slack and Discord are separate switches.

> [!NOTE]
> An agent you started from a chat thread already speaks in that thread, and mentions you when it has a question. It does not also message you on the same platform.

On Discord the bot can only message you if you allow direct messages from members of the server you share with it. A message that cannot be delivered is dropped; the notification is still in Hyperbeam.

To answer a question from a direct message, open the agent’s page with the button. Replies to the bot’s direct message are not passed to the agent.
